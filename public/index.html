About Open Room ETHZ

Unofficial student project. Not affiliated with or endorsed by ETH Zürich. Room availability is based on ETH's published reservation data.

App version –

About availability

Availability is based on ETH's reservation schedule, not actual room occupancy.

Data source

Publicly displayed ETH room allocations, fetched via a read-only proxy. The catalog includes a curated selection of rooms; it is not exhaustive.

Unpublished reservations, restrictions, outages, and room closures may not be represented.

Filters

Filters apply to Free now, Free soon, Rest of day and Browse buildings. Favorites are always shown. Changing a filter never contacts ETH.
